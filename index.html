MAJOR PROJECT 01 ✳

Svastya

Offline Multilingual Health Assistant. A Flutter/Dart Android application for preliminary health awareness with voice and text input in Kannada, Hindi and English. Uses on-device Whisper-Tiny, symptom matching and Logistic Regression to screen 10 common conditions across 86 symptoms. Includes safety gates, adaptive follow-up questions, self-care guidance and offline doctor recommendations. Designed for screening support, not diagnosis.
